# Game Week — Rules & Awards

> Source: [docs/Game_Week_Rules_and_Awards.pdf](docs/Game_Week_Rules_and_Awards.pdf)

Build a game. Ship it. Battle for glory (and cash).

## The Challenge

Build and present a playable game by the end of Game Week. Keep it simple enough to finish — a small, polished game beats a big, broken one.

## Requirements

Every submission must:

- Be playable and demoable in under 5 minutes.

## Judging

Winners are chosen by a combination of staff and student votes.

## Due Date

Thursday at midnight.

## Awards & Prizes

| Category | What it rewards | Prize |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best Overall | The strongest game across the board — top prize of Game Week | $200 |
| Best Visuals / Art Direction | Strongest graphics and visual polish | $100 |
| Most Creative / Fun Gameplay | Best concept and design — rewards fun over polish | $100 |
| Staff Pick / Technical Achievement | Biggest technical stretch — 3D, Unity, multiplayer, etc. | $100 |

Good luck, have fun, make something awesome.
