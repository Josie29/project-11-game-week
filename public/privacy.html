Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026

Neon Strip is a browser game. It is played for fictional chips: no real money is wagered, held, or paid out at any point, and no payment details are ever collected.

You can play without an account

Signing in is optional and the game is fully playable without it. If you never sign in, nothing you do leaves your browser — your bankroll and your character are kept in your own browser's local storage and are not sent anywhere.

What is stored if you do sign in

Signing in with Google lets your progress follow you to another device. Two things are then stored:

  • From your Google account: your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile picture URL. These come from Google when you approve the sign-in, and are used to recognise you when you return and to show who is signed in.
  • Your game save: your chip balance, any marker owed, your character's appearance, which items you own and are wearing, and when the save was last changed.

That is the complete list. No other information about you is collected, and the game asks Google only for basic profile information — nothing else in your Google account is accessible to it.

What is not done

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No cookies beyond the one that keeps you signed in.
  • No email is ever sent to you.

Where it is kept

Account details and saves are stored with Supabase on servers in the United States. The game itself is served as static files by Vercel. Each player's save is readable and writable only by that player, enforced by the database rather than by the game.

Deleting your data

Signing out ends your session but does not delete your save. To have your account and save permanently deleted, contact 01josie@gmail.com and it will be removed. You can also revoke the game's access at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
